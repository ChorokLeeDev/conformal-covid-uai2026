## Issues found during this recheck and their disposition

- **High — operational advice contradicted the recovered negative panel.** The initial recovery draft still retained an appendix deployment table, causal failure definitions and confidence-bound override around the 40% cutoff. Resolution: the final source removes the operational table/causal categories and explicitly withdraws the seed override and deployment interpretation (`paper/main.tex`, historical framework/seed-stability/threshold sections). Fixed-model source-covariate availability is distinguished from the test-aware historical training pipeline. Related-work, baseline and two-point Covertype prose no longer assert prospective superiority. Checked final delta through `3d76028` and final PDF.
- **Medium — attribution methods and mechanism claims were conflated.** MLP concentration used permutation importance while tree concentration used SHAP. Resolution: the MLP table and discussion identify the different estimands, remove the inferred-mechanism column and state that architecture and attribution differences cannot be isolated. Marginal shift summaries and two examples do not identify a causal mechanism.
- **Residual limitation, not repaired by available evidence — original historical experiment ledger missing.** The original 50-seed models, per-record predictions, SHAP results and several external/retraining ledgers remain unavailable. The current-snapshot follow-up, exact stored-model replay and fresh refits do not recover those experiments. The final abstract, recovery section, conclusion and reproducibility section make this distinction. Restoring those exact artifacts and their protocol/software identities is the falsifiable prerequisite for any future claim of reproducing the original experiments.

No new numerical discrepancy was found in the recovered follow-up. The manuscript's explicit small, dependent-task and single-configuration limitations are appropriate. No new deployment threshold, causal explanation, formal statistical power claim or corrected historical significance result is warranted by this review.
